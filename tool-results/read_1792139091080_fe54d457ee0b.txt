     5→## Project
     6→AdAudit — a SaaS that audits blogs for Google AdSense / Ezoic / Mediavine / Raptive approval readiness, shows green/orange/red findings with an approval probability, generates branded PDF reports, and ships content-writer, affiliate-writer, quick-index tools, an affiliate program with withdrawals, subscriptions with BD payment gateways, and a full admin panel at /manage/.
     7→
     8→Stack: Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind 4 + shadcn/ui, Prisma (SQLite sandbox / MySQL-ready for Hostinger), NextAuth v4 (credentials + Google + TOTP 2FA), OpenRouter AI with z-ai fallback, @react-pdf/renderer, cheerio crawler with SSRF guard.
     9→
    10→Design language: single primary color #0d6b4d (deep emerald), no gradients, 6px radius, Open Sans (Telenor @font-face auto-activates if /public/fonts/Telenor-*.woff2 dropped in), Lucide icons (no sparkles/zap/alert icons), 2016-era flat business design. Brand assets in /public/illustrations (8 flat-vector images) and /public/logos (19 real third-party logos with graceful fallback).
